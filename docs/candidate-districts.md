# Candidate pilot districts

**Status: shortlist drafted from general knowledge — every row must be verified before contact.**
Nothing here was checked against a current MEF or MINAM source.

## How to verify (do this first)

1. MEF — *Programa de Incentivos a la Mejora de la Gestión Municipal*: confirm the district's classification
   and whether it has an active solid-waste goal (meta de residuos sólidos) this year.
2. MINAM — SIGERSOL: confirm the district reports waste data and has a source-separation program
   (programa de segregación en la fuente y recolección selectiva).
3. District website: name of the gerencia responsible and a contact.
